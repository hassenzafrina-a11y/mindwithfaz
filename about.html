About

Every session is shaped around your story, not a script.

Fazminna Farook

MSc in Psychological Counselling · BBA (UK) · Dip. in Psychology (USA)

I provide a safe, supportive and non-judgmental space to help you better understand yourself, navigate life's challenges, and work towards emotional wellbeing and personal growth.

Every person who sits across from me carries a different story, and every session is shaped around that story rather than a script. My role isn't to tell you who to be. It's to help you hear yourself clearly enough to find your own way forward, at a pace that feels right for you.

II. The Framework

Understand. Heal. Grow.

Understand

Naming what's actually going on

Before anything changes, it helps to see clearly what you're carrying — without judgment, and without rushing to fix it.

Heal

At a pace that fits your life

Healing isn't linear, and it isn't the same for everyone. We move at whatever speed feels sustainable for you.

Grow

Clarity that holds up after the session ends

The goal isn't just relief in the room — it's building something steadier that stays with you outside of it.
